     cache_eviction_storm/cache (both wrong). Changing only the planted key prefix (sk_live_ -> synth_key_) flipped 2 of 2 tested scenarios.

c) Eligibility is 6/12; bad_deploy and cross_zone_latency have 0 eligible scenarios in the pilot, so those types cannot host faults yet.
d) Pilot latencies of faulted runs are optimistic because of prompt-cache reuse (section 3); this matters for the later latency study.
